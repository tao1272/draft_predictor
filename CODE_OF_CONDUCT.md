# Code of conduct

We want participation in this project to be welcoming and respectful, regardless
of background, experience, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and assume good intent.
- Discuss ideas and code constructively, using concrete feedback.
- Respect other people's privacy and boundaries.
- Accept corrections and make room for different perspectives.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexual harassment, sharing
private information without consent, and repeated disruptive behavior are not
acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact the repository maintainer, [@tao1272](https://github.com/tao1272), to report
a concern. If no private contact channel is available, open an issue requesting
one without including personal information or details of the incident.

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, issue a warning, or restrict
participation depending on the severity and repetition of the behavior. Reports
should be handled discreetly and fairly.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
spaces used to collaborate on this project.
